[basic]
Basic panel:
- temperature: white balance in kelvin. Higher warms the image (more yellow), lower cools it (more blue).
- tint: -150 to +150. Positive adds magenta, negative adds green.
- exposure: in stops (EV). +1.00 is twice as bright; it moves mostly the midtones.
- contrast: -100 to +100. Steepens or flattens the midtones.
- highlights: -100 to +100. Negative recovers bright areas such as skies and skin highlights.
- shadows: -100 to +100. Positive opens up dark areas.
- whites: -100 to +100. Moves the white point; positive makes the brightest tones brighter.
- blacks: -100 to +100. Moves the black point; negative deepens the blacks.
- vibrance: -100 to +100. Boosts muted colours more than saturated ones and protects skin.
- saturation: -100 to +100. Scales every colour equally; use it sparingly.
[tone_curve]
Tone curve, applied after the Basic panel. Each region slider (-100 to +100) bends one part of the tonal range; 5 to 20 is usually plenty:
- curve_highlights: the brightest tones. Negative holds back bright skies and skin; positive makes highlights glow.
- curve_lights: the upper midtones. Positive brightens and adds snap; negative calms them.
- curve_darks: the lower midtones. Negative deepens them; positive opens them.
- curve_shadows: the darkest tones. Positive lifts them toward a matte, faded look; negative makes them denser.
Lights up and darks down is a gentle S-curve: more contrast without clipping either end.
[presence]
Presence (local contrast):
- texture: -100 to +100. Fine detail such as skin pores, foliage and fabric. Negative smooths skin; stay within about 15 either way.
- clarity: -100 to +100. Midtone local contrast: positive adds punch and depth to landscapes and architecture but ages skin, so keep it low or negative on portraits. Rarely beyond +25.
- dehaze: -100 to +100. Positive cuts atmospheric haze and deepens skies; negative adds haze for a soft, dreamy look. Only where the scene asks for it, rarely beyond 20 either way.
[color_mixer]
Color Mixer, per hue band: red, orange (skin), yellow, green (foliage), aqua, blue (sky and water), purple and magenta. List only the bands you change; every other band keeps its value. Per band, -100 to +100:
- hue: shifts the band toward its neighbours (green: negative is more yellow, positive more aqua).
- saturation: how colourful the band is.
- luminance: how bright the band is.
Typical moves: blue luminance -10 to -25 with blue saturation +5 to +15 for a deeper sky; green and yellow saturation -5 to -15 to calm loud foliage; orange luminance +5 to +10 with orange saturation -5 for brighter, softer skin.
[calibration]
Calibration, the camera's base colour rendering for the whole image. Leave it out unless the colour character needs it, and list only what you change (-100 to +100):
- shadow_tint: green (negative) or magenta (positive) in the shadows only.
- red_primary_hue: shifts reds and skin toward orange (positive) or magenta (negative).
- red_primary_saturation: richer or calmer reds and skin.
- green_primary_hue: shifts greens and yellows toward yellow (negative) or cyan (positive).
- green_primary_saturation: richer or calmer greens and yellows.
- blue_primary_hue: shifts blues toward cyan (negative) or purple (positive).
- blue_primary_saturation: richer or calmer colour across the image; +5 to +15 enriches most colours gently.
